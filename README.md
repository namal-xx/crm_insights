# Get AI driven CRM insights 

This is an AI powered CRM dashboard that scores leads and segments customers using Machine learning and Gemini's LLM. You can get follow-up reecommendations and ask questions about your leads by using AI chat interface 

## Overview

### What this app is about?

This app will let you upload their csv's of your customers, leads, and will provide you scored leads, AI-powered recommendations that you follow in order to convert them into your customers, groups of exsting customers (that have similiar behaviours).   

### What problem does this app solves?

This app helps businesses in understanding their customers & leads behaviour. By understanding their behaviours, buisnesses can perform according to their preferencess and can imporve their revenue and quality of their products.
